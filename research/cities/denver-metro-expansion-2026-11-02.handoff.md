## What changed

- Denver adds arc Gateway, Green Mountain and Jewell.
- Boulder adds arc Broomfield and keeps its existing three markets and three vintage/antique records.
- Englewood and Littleton each add the exact already-reviewed Goodwill Archer and arc Bear Valley records.
- Aurora adds arc Iliff and Parker Landing, and reuses nearby reviewed venues inside its 20 km coverage.
- Arvada adds arc Westminster; Westminster reuses that same physical venue and ID.
- Centennial adds arc Centennial and Dry Creek.
- Lakewood reuses Green Mountain and Jewell plus nearby reviewed venues. No duplicate physical IDs were created to increase counts.

The arc locator identifies all newly added arc records as retail store-and-donation locations. Donation-only Arvada, Lakewood, Saddle Rock, Smoky Hill and similar stations were excluded. The current Goodwill directory was reviewed for retail-versus-donation distinctions; this pass did not publish a new Goodwill record because the public Census geocoder was temporarily unreachable. No approximate coordinate was guessed and no failed lookup cooldown or daily ledger was deleted.

## Research use and limits

One shared metro pass used the configured ceiling of **8 web searches** and **24 source-page access attempts**. The local workflow recorded **12 Census geocoder requests** before DNS failure; their one-hour cooldowns and the daily request ledger were preserved. The cached arc operator directory supplied the accepted new pins with zero additional network requests. Publication and validation are offline and used no paid AI, Google Places, Vision scan or marketplace calls.

Market schedules and vintage records reused exact source-reviewed entries where they fall within each city profile; no new season, vendor attendance, live inventory, rating, price or open-now claim was inferred. The new city plan files preserve field-level evidence, source URLs, review dates and coordinate provenance.
